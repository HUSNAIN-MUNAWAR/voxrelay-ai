# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want VoxRelay AI to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming open-source project. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to participate in ways that make collaboration safe and productive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about technical risks.
- Focus feedback on code, behavior, documentation, and outcomes.
- Share context when disagreeing, especially for architecture or security decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Misrepresenting the project’s capabilities, benchmarks, or operational claims.
- Deliberately introducing secrets, malware, or harmful content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and community interactions that do not align with this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Please report serious conduct concerns to `husnainbinmunawar@gmail.com` with enough context for review.
